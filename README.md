# BadgeFor.Me

A satirical static website posing as "Presence-as-a-Service™" — a fictional service that sends Attendance Engineers to scan your badge while you work from home, parodying RTO mandate culture.

**This is satire. No actual badge scanning services are provided.**

Live site: [badgefor.me](https://badgefor.me)

## What it is

BadgeFor.Me presents a fake SaaS product for remote workers navigating return-to-office mandates. Certified Attendance Engineers™ retrieve your badge, drive to your workplace, and execute a precision scan at statistically natural intervals. Your employer's system registers a compliant employee. You register nothing, because you're home.
