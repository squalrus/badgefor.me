The workflow

Four steps to documented presence.

A linear, auditable process. Each stage is handled by a trained professional and logged with timestamp precision.

01

We collect your badge.

A certified Attendance Engineer™ retrieves your badge from a pre-agreed secure location. No eye contact required. A firm handshake is available upon request.

02

We drive to your workplace.

Our CommuteAvoidanceML™ routing engine navigates traffic, construction, and the low-grade existential dread of being in a car at 8:47 a.m. on a Tuesday.

03

We scan your badge.

BadgeScanAI™ selects a statistically natural scan window. Your badge beeps. The reader blinks green. The system logs your "arrival." You are, technically, present.

04

You stay home.

Your badge complies with your RTO mandate. You don't have to. Everyone wins, except the open-plan office you were going to sit in anyway.

Our technology

Purpose-built systems for a single, honest beep.

No fake presence. No simulated activity. Every component below exists to make one badge scan look exactly as routine as it should.

PresenceGPT™

A proprietary large language model trained on HR attendance policy documents, building access schedules, and historical badge-reader congestion data. It determines the statistically optimal moment to perform a scan — not too early, not too late, suspiciously on time.

No fake presence. No simulated activity. Just timing.

BadgeScanAI™

A computer-vision system engineered for one purpose: identify the badge, identify the reader, identify the correct orientation for a clean scan. It randomizes timing and building selection to ensure natural variability across your weekly scan cadence.

It does exactly one thing. It does that one thing very well.

CommuteAvoidanceML™

A machine-learning model that analyzes real-time traffic, predicts construction delays, and optimizes driver routes so your Attendance Engineer arrives on time — without the parking garage standoff or the spiral of wondering if any of this is real.

GeoFaux™ Routing

Not for location spoofing — for driver logistics. Calculates the fastest path between badge pickup and workplace, avoids suspicious patterns like identical arrival times, and ensures scan intervals look exactly like those of a real person who definitely could have taken the elevator.

EthicsBender™

A neural network dedicated to internal compliance review. It flags any process that resembles presence simulation, ensures all operations remain strictly badge-scan-only, and surfaces automated reminders at key moments — including "Don't do crimes" and "This is still satire."

ScanFlow Orchestrator™

The coordination layer that schedules badge pickups, assigns Attendance Engineers, sequences scan operations, and logs each successful beep with timestamp precision.

No fake pings. No dashboard manipulation. Just a beautifully over-engineered system for a single beep.

Dashboard Neutralizer™

A final safeguard ensuring we never interfere with employer systems. No activity generation, no message simulation, no presence fabrication, no data injection of any kind. The only signal your employer receives is the one produced by your real badge touching a real reader.
